# Japanese Path Curriculum

Speak-and-read first. Teach before quiz. Android-first labs sit beside the quest map.

## What is better than tap-only apps

- **Mic coaching** — Web Speech on Android Chrome scores Japanese (kana, kanji, or romaji).
- **Stroke pad** — finger-trace kana and kanji, not only multiple choice.
- **Furigana stories** — tap a word to hear it, then shadow the line.
- **Role-play** — your lines in cafe / first-meet / asking the time.
- **SRS review** — missed items return when they are due.
- **五十音図** — full chart with listen, speak, and trace.
- **Pitch dots** — beginner-friendly high/low mora marks.
- **Speak Life units** — self-intro, numbers, family, food, shopping, daily life, weather.

## Path order (from zero)

1. **START HERE** — speak first phrases, sounds, three scripts, あいうえお
2. **Hiragana** — full chart + dakuten + youon
3. **Katakana** — full chart + dakuten + youon
4. **Kanji** — meaning characters (people, nature, numbers)
5. **Phrases** — greetings and particles
6. **SPEAK LIFE** — everyday speaking/reading lines (unlocked after First phrases)

## Lesson skill order (units after foundations)

1. **Read**
2. **Write** (choice + optional stroke)
3. **Speak** (mic score when available)
4. **Listen**
5. **Match** (line ↔ meaning)

## Gates

- Hiragana after `jp-before-hiragana`
- Katakana after basic hiragana `jp-hiragana-wa-line`
- Kanji after `jp-hiragana-a-line`
- Speak Life after `jp-welcome` so learners can talk on day one
