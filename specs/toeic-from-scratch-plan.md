# TOEIC from Scratch: In-Depth Build Plan

Goal: teach Japanese learners from zero English-for-TOEIC up to **800+** on the official Listening and Reading test. Original items only. UI copy supports EN / JP.

## Pedagogy pillars

1. **VOCAB first** (thematic, high-frequency business English + JP glosses)
2. **READING foundations** (skim emails/notices before exam Parts 5 to 7)
3. **GRAMMAR patterns** (Part 5 / 6 engines)
4. **LISTENING ladder** (sound → Part 1 → 2 → 3 → 4)
5. **EXAM READING** (Part 5 → 6 → 7 single → double/triple)
6. **Full timed practice** + score bands toward 800+

Lesson micro-order inside units when useful: **read → write → speak → listen** (speak supports retention; it is not scored on TOEIC L&R).
