# TOEIC Curriculum (official L&R aligned)

Original practice items only. Modeled on the **TOEIC Listening and Reading** test (not ETS copyrighted forms).

## Official test shape (reference)

| Section | Time | Questions | Parts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | about 45 min | 100 | 1 Photographs (6), 2 Question-Response (25), 3 Conversations (39), 4 Talks (30) |
| Reading | 75 min | 100 | 5 Incomplete Sentences (30), 6 Text Completion (16), 7 Reading Comprehension (54) |

Score scale: 10 to 990 (Listening 5 to 495 + Reading 5 to 495). Product target: **800+**.

## Important accuracy notes

- Standard **TOEIC L&R does not score speaking or writing**. Speaking and Writing are a separate TOEIC S&W test. Lumi's TOEIC path therefore prioritizes listen + read exam skills.
- Study order in the app follows the real paper: Listening Parts 1 to 4, then Reading Parts 5 to 7.
- Part 1 in Lumi uses spoken scene descriptions (original) because we cannot ship ETS photos/audio.
- Within each unit, skill practice still labels Read / Write / Speak / Listen where helpful for learning, but exam claims stay L&R-accurate.
